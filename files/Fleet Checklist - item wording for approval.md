# Fleet Checklist – item wording for approval

For: Pawan (Fleet Operations). Please mark any wording you want changed and send it back; once approved, the new wording goes live without an app update.

## What is changing and why

- **Clearer instructions.** Every item now has a short question and one line telling the driver exactly what to look at and what counts as OK.
- **New order.** The checklist follows how a driver walks the truck: Licence and Odometer, Cabin, Electrical, Tyres, Leaks and Fluid Levels, then Body and Chassis, Safety, General, Accessory Checks, Accident Imagery and Truck Profiles.
- **Yes or No on every item.** The driver must tick Yes or No; nothing is ticked at the start. A **No** needs a short comment saying what is wrong. (For the accident question it is the other way round: **Yes** needs a comment and photos.)
- **More photos.** One photo per tyre, a photo of the weight plate, photos of any new damage, and four photos of the truck (front, rear, left, right).
- **Truck number is locked** to the truck scanned on the home screen, and **Site** must be filled in.
- The separate comment boxes at the end of each section are replaced by the per-item comments and one optional **Other comments** box at the end.

## Weekly items (reviewer only)

Drivers do not see these. When a reviewer opens a checklist, the app shows whether the item has already been done for that truck this week (Monday to Sunday), e.g. "Cabin Tilting inspection conducted on 24/11 by …". If not, the reviewer answers Yes/No.

| Item | What to check | Was |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Cabin tilting | Tilt the cabin, check underneath for leaks, loose parts or damage, then lower and lock it. | New |
| Underneath greasing | Check the grease points under the truck have fresh grease; grease any that are dry. | Greasing Has Been Carried Out to Moving / Rotating Parts |

## Removed

- **Money Box Available in the Truck** – no longer asked. (Past answers are kept.)
- **Front / Rear Windscreens (Free of Cracks)** – replaced by the four separate glass items in Cabin (front windscreen, rear cabin window, driver's window, passenger's window).
- **Section comment boxes** (Engine and Transmission, Electrical, Body and Chassis, Brakes and Tyres, Safety, General, Accessory Checks) – replaced by a comment on each item that is answered No, plus one optional Other comments box.
- The **Engine and Transmission** and **Brakes and Tyres** sections no longer exist as headings; their items moved: oil, coolant, fan belts and air hoses to Leaks and Fluid Levels, PTO to Body and Chassis, brakes to Safety, tyres, wheel nuts, spare and jack to Tyres.
